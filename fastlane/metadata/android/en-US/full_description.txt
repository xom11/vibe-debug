Vibe Debug is a lightweight, zero-internet, zero-ad Android utility for checking and switching USB Debugging, Wireless Debugging, Developer Options, and Keep Awake without digging through nested Settings menus.

Key features:

• Works out of the box (No PC required):
  - Detects if Developer Options is locked and shows an OEM-accurate 2-step unlock guide for Samsung, OPPO / Realme / OnePlus, Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO, Vivo / iQOO, and Pixel / Stock Android, with a direct jump to About Phone / Build Number.
  - Displays live status for USB Debugging, Wireless Debugging (Android 11+), Developer Options, and Keep Awake, and highlights the exact toggle inside System Settings.

• 1-Tap Mode (with WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS):
  - Grant WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS once via ADB or Shizuku (including the reinstall-with-grant command for ColorOS / OxygenOS 15–16+) to unlock instant 1-tap switching inside the app, across 2 focused Quick Settings tiles (USB Debug & Wireless Debug), and via 1 adaptive multi-size Home Screen widget (scaling from compact 1×1 / 2×1 icon controls to labeled 3×1 / 4×1 / 2×2 / 4×2 cards).
  - Optionally keeps the screen awake while USB Debugging is active and clearly warns you before turning on Wireless Debugging on mobile data (5G/4G) without Wi-Fi.

• Clean Launch (Turn Off Debug & Launch):
  - Pick banking, digital ID, e-wallet, enterprise, or security-sensitive apps that refuse to run while Developer Options is active. Launching them from Shortcuts or a badged 1×1 home shortcut turns off Developer Options cleanly first.

• Auto Turn-Off for Selected Apps (optional, needs 1-Tap Mode):
  - Enable "Auto-turn off when opening apps" and grant Usage Access, and opening one of your selected apps from its regular icon turns debugging off automatically. Choose per app whether to turn off all of Developer Options or only USB & Wireless Debugging, and whether debugging comes back on a few seconds after you leave.
  - Off by default. While enabled it runs as a visible foreground service with a persistent notification and a Stop button, pauses while the screen is off, and never uses Accessibility. Foreground app checks happen only on your device and are never stored.

• Safety Auto-Off Timer:
  - Automatically turn off USB Debugging after 15, 30, or 60 minutes using a battery-friendly, inexact system alarm (no exact-alarm permission needed).

• Troubleshooting:
  - Copy device diagnostics (device model, Android version, permission and debug-setting states) to the clipboard to paste into a bug report. Nothing is sent automatically.

• Privacy by Design:
  - No INTERNET permission, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. Everything stays on your device. Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later.
